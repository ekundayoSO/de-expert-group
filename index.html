About Us

DE-Expert Group Ltd is a UK-based organisation specialising in educational support and health and wellbeing services. Our focus is on helping individuals, students, families, educational institutions, and communities access practical support that encourages learning, personal development, health awareness, and wellbeing. We believe that quality education and improved wellbeing are essential foundations for stronger individuals and thriving communities.

Our Mission

To deliver accessible, inclusive, reliable, and high-quality educational and health support services that empower individuals, enhance learning outcomes, promote wellbeing, and contribute positively to healthier and better-informed communities. We are committed to professionalism, integrity, compassion, continuous improvement, and excellence in everything we do.

Our Vision

To become a trusted and respected UK organisation, recognised for excellence, reliability, and innovation in delivering high-quality educational and health support services. We aspire to empower individuals, promote lifelong learning, improve wellbeing, and make meaningful contributions to healthier, more inclusive communities.
